ServerNet Studio Capture 0.1.0
Licensed AGPL-3.0-only. Corresponding source https://github.com/servernetir/servernet-studio

Extract this ZIP into a folder. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, and choose Load unpacked.
Select the extracted folder. Sign in to https://studio.servernet.cloud and open the Chrome tab you want to share.
Click ServerNet Studio Capture, capture the visible area, review the preview, then choose Attach in Studio.
The screenshot is attached locally to your draft. You decide when to submit it to the selected vision model.
This preview extension is distributed directly by ServerNet.
